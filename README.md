## About the Game

**THE FINAL HIRE** is a narrative-driven investigation game set inside a company operating under pressure.

You play as **Alice**, the HR recruitment officer working under the company's CEO, **Aditya**.

Your office is your investigation room. Resumes, computers, phone calls, and identity documents contain pieces of information about the people applying to join the company.

Your task is to examine the available evidence and make careful decisions.

A candidate may look perfect on paper but hide important contradictions. Another may appear suspicious while having a reasonable explanation.

The challenge is to distinguish meaningful evidence from assumptions.

## Story Premise

The company needs the right people, but every hiring decision carries consequences.

A careless hire can damage the company. Rejecting the wrong person can also have a cost.

As Alice, you work through candidate files, investigate details, and decide what should happen next.

Meanwhile, the office atmosphere and messages from within the company suggest that there may be more happening than an ordinary recruitment process.

**Can you make the right decision when the evidence is incomplete?**

## Gameplay Direction

The intended recruitment gameplay follows an evidence-based investigation process:

1. Read a candidate's resume.
2. Compare relevant details with available documents.
3. Investigate employment, education and references.
4. Record contradictions and supporting evidence.
5. Make a hiring decision.

The game uses fictional candidate identities and fictional document information.

## Installation & Running

### Requirements

- Godot Engine 4.7.2
- Windows / compatible development environment

### Steps

1. Clone this repository:

   ```bash
   git clone YOUR_REPOSITORY_URL
   ```

2. Open Godot Project Manager.
3. Click **Import**.
4. Select the `project.godot` file.
5. Open the project.
6. Press **F5** to run the game.
